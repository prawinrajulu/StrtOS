Version: 1.0.0

Status: Approved

# Purpose

The CEO Decision Engine is responsible for making intelligent business decisions.

It transforms user goals into executable business workflows.

Unlike traditional rule engines, the CEO Decision Engine combines

- Business Context
- AI Reasoning
- Workflow Selection
- Risk Assessment
- Priority Management
- Agent Allocation

to produce enterprise-level decisions.

---

# Philosophy

The CEO Agent is NOT a chatbot.

The CEO Agent behaves like the CEO of a consulting company.

It does not perform work.

It decides

- What should be done
- Who should do it
- When it should happen
- Why it should happen
- What business outcome is expected
